Artist Biography

Joshua Brown makes audio visual work, sculptural objects and sometimes combines the two[cite: 2]. Themes that recur in his work are sustainability, memory, and deterioration[cite: 2]. The construction of his sculptural pieces is carefully considered, with reuse and recyclability being important factors in his choice of materials[cite: 2]. Joshua’s audio-visual work is influenced by sample culture[cite: 2]. He is currently based in County Clare, Ireland and London, pursuing his MA in Sculpture at the Royal College of Art, Battersea[cite: 2].

Artist Statement

My practice considers multimedia art an act of spatial and temporal translation. Blending heavy physical media—such as cast metals, stone, and industrial timber—with time-based digital formats, I examine how physical boundaries and digital codecs dictate human perception. The handle av.h.264 serves as a conceptual nod to this framework: a recognition of how raw, monumental physical reality becomes compressed, formatted, and transmitted through digital channels[cite: 1, 2].

Central to my work is the loop—both as a physical structural motif and a temporal cinematic device. I investigate the friction that occurs when permanent, unyielding industrial materials are paired with the transient, fleeting nature of moving images or soundscapes[cite: 2].
