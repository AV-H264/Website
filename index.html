Artist Biography

Joshua Brown makes audio visual work, sculptural objects and sometimes combines the two. Themes that recur in his work are sustainability, memory, and deterioration. The construction of his sculptural pieces is carefully considered, with reuse and recyclability being important factors in his choice of materials. Joshua’s audio-visual work is influenced by sample culture. He is currently based in County Clare, Ireland and London, pursuing his MA in Sculpture at the Royal College of Art, Battersea.

Artist Statement

My practice considers multimedia art an act of spatial and temporal translation. Blending heavy physical media—such as cast metals, stone, and industrial timber—with time-based digital formats, I examine how physical boundaries and digital codecs dictate human perception. The handle av.h.264 serves as a conceptual nod to this framework.
